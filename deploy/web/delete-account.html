Account & Data Rights

Delete Your Palora Account

You have full, unconditional control over your data. Follow the simple steps below to permanently delete your account and all associated records.

Option 1: In the App (Instant Self-Serve)

If you have the Palora app installed on your Android or iOS device, you can delete your account immediately:

1

Open Palora & Go to Profile

Open the app, make sure you are signed in, and tap the Profile tab in the bottom bar.

2

Open Settings

Tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner of your profile.

3

Tap “Delete account”

Scroll down to the Danger Zone section and select Delete account.

4

Confirm Deletion

Confirm the prompt. Your session ends immediately, and your account data is wiped.

Immediate Hard Delete: Your profile is unlinked, mutual matches are removed, and media files are purged from storage right away.

Option 2: Email Request (If App Is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled Palora, lost access to your device, or cannot log in, email our support team directly. We will verify account ownership and process the deletion within 48 hours.

Send Deletion Request Email

Direct email: support@kraftisium.xyz (Subject: Palora Account Deletion Request)

What Gets Deleted

When an account is deleted, our backend executes a cascading database purge:

Data Category Specific Items Action
Authentication & Identity Email, password hash, phone number, OAuth links Permanently Erased
Profile Information Name, bio, gender, DOB, occupation, lifestyle, city Permanently Erased
Photos & Media All uploaded profile pictures and story videos Purged from Storage
Social & Matches Likes, passes, mutual matches, chat conversations, messages Permanently Erased
Device Information Push notification tokens and active sessions Revoked & Deleted
Safety Records Harassment reports or abuse bans (if applicable) Retained for Legal Compliance

Delete Specific Data (Without Deleting Account)

You can remove individual parts of your profile at any time while keeping your account active:

  • • Photos: Open Profile → Edit Profile and tap the × on any photo to permanently delete it.
  • • Bio & Preferences: Clear or edit any profile field (hometown, company, university, habits).
  • • Unmatch: Open any chat thread and select Unmatch to erase the conversation and match link.
