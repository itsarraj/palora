1. Commitment & Scope

Palora (“we”, “our”, or “us”), operated by Kraftisium (https://palora.kraftisium.xyz), respects and protects the personal privacy of our community members. This Privacy Policy discloses exactly how we collect, handle, store, and purge your information when using the Palora mobile application and our backend-for-frontend services.

Our fundamental pledge: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or third-party marketers. We do not incorporate external ad networks or tracking SDKs.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver real-time social discovery and dating features, Palora collects specific categories of user data:

Account & Identity

Full name, email address, password hash, date of birth (enforces 18+ policy), and optional phone number or Google Sign-In ID.

Photos & Profile

Profile photos uploaded from camera or photo library, bio, occupation, university, relationship goals, lifestyle traits, and interests.

Location Data

Approximate and fine location accessed only with your runtime consent while using Discover to compute distance to nearby matches. Never tracked in background.

Chats & Matches

Swipes (likes/passes), mutual matches, private 1-on-1 chat messages, feed posts, daily stories, and user blocks/reports.

Device & Push Tokens

FCM/APNS push notification tokens for match/message alerts, IP address for security, abuse detection, and rate limiting.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Nearby Matching & Discovery: To show you compatible profiles in your city and calculate relative distance in kilometers/miles.
  • Messaging & Social Connection: To allow real-time private communication between users who have mutually liked each other.
  • Community Safety & Moderation: To verify users are 18+, protect members from impersonation, and process safety reports and blocks.
  • Push Notifications: To alert you instantly when you receive a new like, mutual match, or chat message (customizable in your phone's notification settings).

4. Information Sharing

Your profile (first name, age, city, distance, bio, photos, and selected interests) is visible only to other Palora users matching your discovery filters.

  • Private 1-on-1 Messages: Chat messages are visible solely to you and your matched partner.
  • Infrastructure Partners: We work strictly with enterprise infrastructure providers (database hosting, push notification gateways, SMS/email OTP delivery) bound by non-disclosure agreements.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We never share or sell personal data to advertisers, aggregators, or third-party marketing services.
