Zero Tolerance Policy

Child Safety Standards

Palora’s explicit policy and mandatory standards against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

1. Zero Tolerance for CSAE & CSAM

Palora maintains an uncompromising, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), online grooming, trafficking, and any form of endangerment of minors.

Absolute Prohibition: Any user found to upload, generate, transmit, facilitate, solicit, or possess material involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors is immediately and permanently banned from Palora. All relevant data is preserved for and reported directly to law enforcement authorities.

2. Strictly 18+ Community & Age Enforcement

Palora is an adults-only dating and friendship platform. We explicitly prohibit registration by anyone under the age of 18.

  • Mandatory Date-of-Birth Verification: Every user must declare their date of birth during onboarding; anyone under 18 is prevented from creating an account.
  • Proactive Account Termination: If an account is discovered to belong to or represent a minor, it is permanently closed immediately.

3. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Palora provides multiple direct, in-app mechanisms for members to report safety concerns, suspicious accounts, or inappropriate content:

  • 1-Tap Profile Reporting: While viewing any profile or swipe card, tap the Report icon, choose the reason (such as “Underage user” or “Inappropriate content”), and submit.
  • Direct Chat Reporting: Inside any conversation thread, tap the options menu (…) and select Report & Block.
  • Urgent Review Queue: Reports categorized under child safety or underage users are routed to our highest-priority moderation queue for immediate investigation.

4. Reporting to Authorities & NCMEC

Palora complies fully with all applicable international, regional, and national child safety laws, including:

  • NCMEC Reporting: In compliance with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and global safety standards, we report any identified or suspected instances of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and regional Cyber Crime divisions.
  • Law Enforcement Cooperation: We preserve relevant logs, IP addresses, and records and cooperate fully with official legal requests and law enforcement investigations.
